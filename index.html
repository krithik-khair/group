About Me

Krithik Khair is a visionary entrepreneur and technologist leading a diverse portfolio of companies under the Khair Group umbrella. With expertise spanning web development, mobile applications, research, publishing, and creative production, I have established a comprehensive ecosystem of interconnected ventures.

Through Krixon Digital, we deliver cutting-edge web solutions and digital platforms. KHAIR A&G focuses on educational mobile applications and engaging games that combine learning with entertainment. Our research division, KHAIR PRESEARCH, contributes to academic discourse through publications on platforms like SSRN and ORCID.

The Kritvya Doctrine represents our philosophical framework, guiding ethical decision-making and personal development. KHAIR PUBLICATIONS provides professional book publishing services, while KP PRODUCTIONS creates original music compositions and audio experiences.

My multidisciplinary approach combines technical excellence with creative thinking, resulting in innovative solutions that address complex challenges across various domains. Through strategic leadership and a commitment to ethical innovation, we're building an interconnected ecosystem that advances technology, knowledge, and creative expression.
